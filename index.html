      rules
      
chaser wins by stepping onto the runner.
runner wins by touching all four corner hexes.
tie: same position 3 times, neither side able to act, or 240 turns.

one action per turn, runner first:
1 move   up to 2 single steps. facing = last step.
2 place  a rune within 2 hexes.
3 type   change type of a rune within 1.
4 str    bump strength (1-3) of a rune within 2.
5 remove rune on the hex to your left (60 deg left of facing). wall loses 1 durability instead.

no runes on corners, the center, or on a player.
every rune affects both players. buff runes have cooldowns.
frozen: cannot move, can still place/modify/remove.

B bomb      hidden 6-hex zone, drawn by the placer. whoever walks in is frozen for strength turns. spent on use. a rune placed in a zone, or a zone drawn over a rune, destroys both. one per player.
D dash      +2+strength steps.
P push      shoved strength hexes along the arrow, no triggers on the way or on landing.
T teleport  jump to another teleport, +strength steps.
W wall      blocks movement. strength = durability.
G grab      stepper's move ends, next strength moves are 1 step.
H return    go to the center. higher strength = shorter cooldown.
