rules
Chaser wins by stepping onto the runner. Runner wins by touching all four corner flags. Tie: same position 3 times, nobody can act, or 400 turns. One action per turn, runner first. Click your piece to open the action ring: move: up to 2 single steps. Facing = last step. place: put a rune within 2 hexes. type: change the type of a rune within 1. strength: bump a rune (1-3) within 2. remove: take the rune on the hex 60 degrees left of your facing (dashed marker). A wall loses 1 durability instead. No runes on corners, the center, or on a player. Every rune affects both players; buff runes have cooldowns. Frozen players cannot move but can still place, modify and remove. One bomb per player.
